## B. Photography

Real photographs only: the Gately factory, the retread line, technicians and
branded vehicles. Every empty slot on the site shows its name and a brief for
the shot. About 40 slots (each tread and landing page has its own); the priority ones are:

1. `home-hero`: the retread line with a technician at work
2. `about-hero`: factory exterior with signage and vehicles
3. `fleet-hero`: the collection truck at a customer depot
4. `tread-*`: one straight-on picture of each of the nine tread patterns (Bandag can usually supply these)
5. `casing-accept`, `casing-reject` and the six reject-condition close-ups
6. `card-*`, `retreads-*`, `repairs-earthmover`, `rema-tip-top`

A half-day shoot at the plant by a local photographer covers almost all of it.
Landscape, at least 1600 px wide. Get written permission from any customer
whose vehicles or premises appear.

## C. Brand assets

- The official Bandag dealer logo artwork (the header currently sets the name in type) and confirmation from Bandag SA of how a franchise may use it
- Supplier logos (Apollo, Dunlop, Sumitomo, Rema Tip Top) if they are to appear, with permission

## D. Documents for the resource library

PDFs, named as listed in `src/data/resources.ts`: Bandag product guide,
Application Specific tread selector, Bandag PSI overview, Dunlop, Sumitomo and
Apollo range guides, Rema Tip Top product guide, fleet owner health and safety
guide, driver daily tyre check card, warranty terms, SANS 20109 certificate.
Until a file is supplied its entry offers "Request by email".

## E. Proof (phase 2, but start collecting now)

- Two or three **case studies with real numbers**: fleet, sector, fleet size, tyre cost per kilometre before and after, over what period. Written consent to be named
- **Testimonials** from named people at named fleets
- **Team**: names, roles, direct numbers and portraits of the people customers deal with

## F. Stock and pricing data (phase 2)

- Who can export the stocked sizes each week, and from what system
- Whether indicative prices may be published

## G. Ownership

- **Who owns the site** at East London Bandag: one named person
- **Who receives form emails**, and who covers when they are away
- **Who answers WhatsApp**, and during what hours
- **Who maintains it**: the builder on a small retainer, or a named staff member comfortable editing the data files
- Access to the domain registrar and the Google Business Profile
